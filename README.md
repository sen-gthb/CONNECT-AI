# CONNECT-AI

CONNECT-AI is a faculty AI literacy initiative at the University of Utah College of Nursing. It gives faculty a shared foundation in AI, then helps each person apply it to their own teaching, scholarship, and leadership.

**Watch the videos:** [https://sen-gthb.github.io/CONNECT-AI/](https://sen-gthb.github.io/CONNECT-AI/)

## How the course is organized

Everyone starts with the same foundations, then chooses the applied modules that fit their role.

| Module | Focus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome: Start Here | Welcome video and getting set up | In development |
| Module 1: Behind the Curtain | What AI is, how it works, what it does well, and where it goes wrong | Lessons 1.1–1.9 available |
| Module 2: Using AI Well and Responsibly | Approved tools and privacy, prompting, checking outputs, safeguards for high-stakes work | In development |
| Module 3: AI in Teaching and Student Mentorship | Syllabus language, assignment policies, guiding students' AI use | Planned |
| Module 4: AI in Course Design and Innovation | Case-based learning, simulation, assessment | Planned |
| Module 5: AI in Scholarship and Research | Literature, analysis support, writing, research integrity | Planned |
| Module 6: AI in Leadership and Governance | Evaluating tools and vendor claims, workforce impact, governance | Planned |
| Module 7: Staying Current with AI | Emerging topics and shared examples | Planned |

Modules are made up of short lessons. Most lessons include a video of about 3 to 5 minutes and a brief hands-on activity.

## Approach

CONNECT-AI is built with faculty, for faculty, using human-centered design. It draws on several published frameworks:

- **AI-ABCs** (Kobeissi, Santa Maria, & Park, 2025): the content framework for Module 1. CONNECT-AI adds its own elements, including the four families of AI: symbolic, machine learning, generative, and agentic.
- **AI literacy constructs** (Wang, Rau, & Yuan, 2023): awareness, use, evaluation, and ethics, as adapted for nursing leadership by Chamblee (2026). CONNECT-AI defines these for nurse faculty and uses them to shape and evaluate the course.
- **Stanford Teaching Commons AI literacy framework** (n.d.): informs Module 2 and Module 3.
- **CLEAR framework** (Lo, 2023): guides the prompting lesson in Module 2.

Throughout, the course emphasizes that nurses belong at every stage of AI, from design to evaluation (Ronquillo et al., 2021).

## Who it's for

Faculty and educators in nursing and the health sciences. No technical background is needed.
