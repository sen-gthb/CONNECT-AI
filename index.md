# CONNECT-AI
## Module 1: Behind the Curtain

Short videos on what AI is, how it works, what it does well, and where it goes wrong. Each one runs about five minutes. Watch them in order, or jump to the topic you need.

---

### [Lesson 1.1: Behind the Curtain: Introduction](https://share.synthesia.io/ce3ffa3b-14f2-4da8-919f-d29e14da2245)

Introduces nurses and nurse educators to AI in health care through a medication-safety example, showing why polished AI output still needs human scrutiny. Explains the basics of how generative AI works, its risks and benefits, and introduces the AI-ABCs framework for using it responsibly.

### [Lesson 1.2: What Does "AI" Mean?](https://share.synthesia.io/c1abed48-f3fd-4b62-8ada-f456a532fbf5)

Introduces what people mean by "AI," contrasts human and artificial intelligence, and lays out the four major AI families (symbolic, machine learning, generative, and agentic) for a general audience. Viewers learn how to tell these approaches apart and where human judgment still matters most.

### [Lesson 1.3: Symbolic AI](https://share.synthesia.io/26020ecb-0832-4eb5-8920-c942a6a7463e)

Explains symbolic, rule-based AI for health care learners: how if-then rules drive alerts and recommendations, where systems like MYCIN and drug-interaction checks are used, and why they miss novel cases and generate alert fatigue.

### [Lesson 1.4: Machine Learning](https://share.synthesia.io/a54528b5-fe23-4388-8e20-9f9c062e98c0)

Explains machine learning for a general audience, using spam filters, face unlock, and health care examples to show how models learn from examples, make predictions, and can fail outside the data they were trained on.

### [Lesson 1.5: Generative AI](https://share.synthesia.io/a7e7d33d-95c8-42a0-b1c1-d685e98a9709)

Explains generative AI and large language models for beginners, showing how chatbots learn to produce text, images, and other content, and where they can go wrong with hallucinations and people-pleasing answers.

### [Lesson 1.6: Agentic AI](https://share.synthesia.io/f9ed8a4f-ed07-4b34-81cd-0008199aa36e)

Explains agentic AI for general viewers, showing how goal-driven systems use tools in a plan-act-check loop to complete tasks like research and workflow automation. Also highlights key risks such as overreach and prompt injection, and when human oversight is needed.

### [Lesson 1.7: How AI Learns](https://share.synthesia.io/1ec55498-65df-4b75-a812-330949ffd2a2)

Explains the main ways AI systems learn (supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement, and self-supervised learning) using familiar human and clinical examples. Built for health care professionals, it shows what chatbots learn from and how to judge AI outputs more critically.

### [Lesson 1.8: AI Benefits](https://share.synthesia.io/dde641a7-12b3-4f4b-bf4e-c04e4446e649)

Explores AI's benefits and practical uses for health care and academic work, showing educators and clinicians how it can speed drafting, summarizing, analysis, and patient support. Ends with a simple hands-on prompt for trying Copilot, ChatGPT, or Gemini on everyday tasks.

### [Lesson 1.9: Bias](https://share.synthesia.io/f4a7d8e7-edc1-4854-88f4-d63586c86125)

Explains how AI bias arises across symbolic, machine learning, generative, and agentic systems, with health care examples and practical questions for clinicians to spot unfair outcomes. Shows how proxy measures, missing data, and unchecked deployment can disadvantage patients, and how to reduce bias.
